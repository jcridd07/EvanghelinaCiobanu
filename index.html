About

A life in music

Dr. Evanghelina Ciobanu is a Moldovan-born cellist known for her rich tone, thoughtful interpretation, and commanding stage presence. An accomplished soloist, chamber musician, and orchestral player, she has performed across Europe, the United States, and South Korea. Her career spans notable orchestral appointments—including principal roles with the National Symphony Orchestra “Tele-radio Moldova” and the Wonderful Youth Orchestra—as well as appearances with the South Florida Symphony Orchestra and the Chamber Orchestra of the International Center for Music.

Evanghelina holds a Doctor of Musical Arts in Cello Performance from the Peabody Institute of The Johns Hopkins University, where she studied under Alan Stepansky. She previously completed her master’s degree at the University of Cincinnati College-Conservatory of Music (CCM) under Ilya Finkelshteyn and her bachelor’s degree at Park University’s International Center for Music under Daniel Veis. Over her career, she has garnered accolades at international competitions, prestigious festival fellowships—including the Sitka International Cello Seminar and the Castleman Quartet Program—and civic honors such as Peabody’s Georgie Award.

Deeply dedicated to education and community engagement, Evanghelina has served on the faculties and educational programs of the Peabody Institute, Baltimore School for the Arts, BSO OrchKids, Cincinnati School of Music, and KC Music Academy. Her teaching focuses on building healthy technique, expressive musicality, and a lifelong devotion to the instrument.

Now based in Salt Lake City, she is available for solo recitals, chamber collaborations, private events, and cello instruction both in person and online.

Philosophy

Teaching Approach

Fostering musical expression, healthy mechanics, and artistic confidence.

Every student brings a unique voice, natural musical intuition, and individual goals to the cello. My teaching centers on creating a supportive, encouraging environment where curiosity and discipline work hand-in-hand to cultivate lifelong artistic growth.

A primary foundation of my pedagogy is physical ease and ergonomic efficiency. By prioritizing tension-free posture, balanced weight distribution, and natural body alignment, students develop both endurance and a rich, resonant tone while preventing injury.

Beyond technical precision, lessons emphasize creative musical interpretation, active listening, and thoughtful score analysis. Whether preparing for auditions, developing ensemble skills, or discovering the cello for the first time, students learn to approach the instrument with curiosity, resilience, and personal expression.
